# Research and target decision

Checked 17 November 2026. No customer data is shipped. The sample research desk, suppliers, permissions and capture results are fictional.

## Bill and fit

[Import.io pricing](https://www.import.io/pricing) lists Advanced at US$899 month-to-month, equivalent to US$10,788 across twelve monthly subscriptions. Its annual-term rate is US$699 per month. The comparison must show the cheaper term too. These are list prices, not a verified NZ or AU customer invoice. Local customer and CRM repository searches supplied no corroborating subscription evidence. Live CRM was not read. Buyer fit is an inference for research and distribution operators.

Scores in charter order: annual bill 2, data-shaped 4, buyer fit 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 5, import path 5. Total 21/30. The family is web extraction, absent from the registry at selection. This is distinct from moving existing databases between systems, product-feed publishing and network uptime monitoring.

Diffbot Plus scored 19/30 (2,3,2,4,3,5). Its [US$899 plan](https://www.diffbot.com/pricing) qualifies on published annualised spend, but its purchased knowledge graph and automatic classification are a poorer fit for this build. ParseHub scored 19/30 (1,4,2,3,4,5), and its [US$599 Professional monthly price](https://www.parsehub.com/pricing) fails the five-figure bill filter. Visualping scored 20/30 (1,4,2,3,5,5), but its [public plans](https://visualping.io/pricing) do not establish a qualifying annual bill. Neither was registered.

Alternative searches returned comparison and directory pages, including [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/import-io-2017-12-19/competitors/alternatives) and [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/import-io/). This establishes search competition, not measured query volume. No volume or ranking promise is made.

## Export and operating evidence

- [Getting the data](https://docs.import.io/user-guide/tutorial/part-6/): completed crawl-run data can be downloaded as CSV, Excel or NDJSON.
- [Dashboard](https://docs.import.io/reference/extractors/managing/dashboard/): completed runs and downloadable data are separate from extractor management.
- [Creating a listing extractor](https://docs.import.io/user-guide/tutorial/part-1/): records, fields and repeatable extraction are the practical unit of work.
- [Managing an interactive extractor](https://docs.import.io/reference/extractors/interactive/managing-interactive/): input lists have a separate export/import path. This build does not claim to import those definitions from a data CSV.

## Scope

The owned base implements static-page extraction, snapshot history, field checks, changes, owner follow-ups, source permission review and approved feed output. It does not reproduce the vendor's browser fleet, proxy network, automated detection, managed service or pricing-intelligence application. Extra collection methods must be scoped and tested in the customised version. No unverified rival capability or saving is claimed.
